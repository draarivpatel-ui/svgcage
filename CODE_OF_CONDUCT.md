# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep participation respectful, factual, and focused on technical merit. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Give clear technical feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Keep security reports private until a fix or disclosure plan exists.
- Respect user privacy and never publish another person's personal data.
- Credit prior work accurately and do not misrepresent test results.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, revert changes, or restrict participation when this code is violated. Serious or repeated violations may be reported to the hosting platform.

## Reporting

Use the private security or contact route listed in `SECURITY.md` when a concern involves safety, exploit details, or private information. Otherwise open a civil public issue without personal attacks.
